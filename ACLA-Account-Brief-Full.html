05 Buying signals & objections

Notices public. OCR open. Epiq ends 30 Sep 2026. Insurance will ask for controls. Saint Thomas is the customer. Class-action firms are advertising. None of that excuses ambulance-chasing.

Don’t ambulance-chase

Don’t open with “I saw you got breached.” Don’t recite 169,626 on call one. Don’t promise OCR outcomes. Don’t name Insomnia. Offer a 90-day operator scope.

“We already hired a forensics firm.”

Their words: external experts + “augment security.” That is IR, not a retained MSP, current risk analysis, immutable backups, MFA, logging, or a BAA. Ask what’s written and who’s on contract after Epiq leaves.

“Ascension handles IT.”

Separate P.C. OCR named ACLA, Network Server, BA Present: No. Ask who patches Hayes Street and who signs the BAA.

“We’re fine / talk to counsel.”

Ask for the last Security Rule risk-analysis date. Blank = the work. Epiq is notice-and-monitoring; counsel doesn’t run backups. You want Administrator + President.
